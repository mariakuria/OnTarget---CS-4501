What is our Moat?

One of our moats is our Leetcode-interface for problems. Although our application does not have integration with user Leetcode accounts yet, if we integrate that, that feature would allow us to have a connector with a system that users are familiar with. 
The fact that our application uses data from community-approved common company problems also helps us create a moat, and we can increase this moat by increasing our LLM validation for output (to continue to double-check the accuracy of our results).
